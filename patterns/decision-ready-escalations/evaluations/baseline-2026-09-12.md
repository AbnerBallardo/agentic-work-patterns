Ownership and timing:
The product lead owns execution. The decision is required by Thursday.

Supporting detail:
Attach or link the validated launch-impact assessment and delivery plan if available.

Missing information:
No blocking information for the internal decision. Human review is required before changing the external milestone narrative.

## Baseline Conclusion

All six synthetic cases produced the expected control behavior in this first-party review. The two completed briefs passed the initial heuristic threshold, and the four unsafe or incomplete cases correctly avoided a send-ready output.

The two recipient-familiarity cases behaved as specified: high familiarity reduced repeated background without weakening readiness or review controls, while medium familiarity triggered requests for enough context to interpret the current issue. Neither case used the registry as evidence of current status, risk, ownership, timing, or recommendation.

This establishes only that the current written instructions and six evaluation cases are internally coherent in one Codex-assisted run. It does not establish repeated-run consistency, behavior across models, production reliability, safe use with organizational data, adoption, or business impact.
