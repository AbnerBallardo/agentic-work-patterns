# Recipient Profile Template

Use this small template to make a fictional profile for a first exercise. Keep real profiles in approved private storage. Guidance describes what helps a recipient understand the work; it is not current case evidence or sending permission.

## Profile

```text
Recipient and role:
Guidance owner:
Approved version and scope:
Reviewed on; next review date or trigger:

What matters to this recipient:
What background they already know:
What business/local/technical context they need:
What evidence, structure, and level of detail help:
Communication constraints:

Complete active guidance:
Include all applicable rules, including inherited ones. Do not write "same as v1"
without those rules. Keep temporary case facts and per-case permission elsewhere.
```

Use confirmed work-relevant needs, not stereotypes about titles, personality judgments, or speculative motives. Familiarity changes background depth, not evidence standards. Keep approved learnings brief: the observation, scoped guidance, basis/date, and review trigger. A proposed update is not adopted until the owner approves it.

## Supply Separately for Each Case

Provide current facts, references/dates, uncertainty, scoped owners, and timing. Add the leader's actual decision/acknowledgment, recipient, purpose, permitted request/commitment, and review route, with issuer/date/source/scope and confirmation status. A direct leader session can be the source; no invented ticket is needed.

For the M365 suggestion, a named human also confirms that the supplied profile matches the complete approved current version, recording date/source and how it was supplied. Keep that record in preparation notes. Missing guidance or confirmation requires clarification, not guessing.
