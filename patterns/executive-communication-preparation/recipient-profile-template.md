## Per-Communication Direction

Supply this separately for each session; do not infer it from the profile:

- **Case identifier or clear situation description:**
- **Issuing leader and role:**
- **Direction issued on:** Date; include time and timezone when timing affects applicability.
- **Instruction source/reference:** Approved message, record, or direct leader session; provide a permitted locator or minimal excerpt without inventing an identifier.
- **Confirmation status:** Direct leader instruction / confirmed against source by [human, date] / unconfirmed. Do not claim independent authentication.
- **Scope and applicability:** Covered case, intended recipient, permitted purpose, limits, and any expiry, superseding instruction, or return trigger.
- Leader's decision or acknowledgment and purpose of escalation.
- Intended recipient and awareness, action, or decision requested.
- Current strategic direction, constraints, and permitted commitments.
- Review route: sender review followed by direct sending, or leader review before sending.
- Any additional required approvals and conditions that require returning to the leader.
- Current case facts, evidence, unknowns, owners with their supported site/service/action scope, and timing.
- Blocking information and required human reviews recorded separately; apply readiness precedence from the agent instructions. Do not expand an owner's scope when impact changes.

A profile's approval is not per-case delegation. The instruction reference provides traceability, not proof of identity or authority. Missing or disputed provenance, superseded direction, or a case outside the recorded scope requires human confirmation; an unsupported team assertion cannot establish direct-send readiness. A direct leader instruction in an approved session needs no invented external approval record.

Approved guidance must still preserve supported facts and uncertainty. A request from the owner to omit a material fact is a conflict to resolve, not an exception to that boundary.

This template does not establish committee or presentation support. Those remain future extensions of the draft pattern.
