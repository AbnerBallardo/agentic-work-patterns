# Suggested Use of the M365 Copilot Agent

- **Status:** Suggested user guidance; not tenant-tested. No tenant testing is planned by the author.

This describes how a future adopter could use the suggested agent after local approval and checks. It does not imply an agent is available. Any exploratory use should begin with fictional cases.

## Start a Separate Preparation

During controlled tenant testing, use only the approved test agent, named test accounts, and fictional cases/profiles within the documented test window. Do not send operational messages even if an output says Ready for sender review; test sharing is not pilot authorization. Follow the [test-access procedure](build-guide.md#control-test-sharing-and-pilot-access).

Select the approved agent and start a new conversation for a new case or recipient. Identify the intended CEO or regional CIO explicitly. Shared use does not imply shared conversation memory, approved guidance adoption, or transfer of the previous recipient's permissions.

The agent helps ask questions and prepare text. It does not verify source truth, decide, approve, send, contact other areas, or update profiles.

## Supply the Case Package

Provide only information permitted in this environment:

- Complete current recipient guidance, or its approved source and expected version for retrieval/completeness checking. Include owner, review date/trigger, and all active rules.
- What happened, why it matters, business consequences, necessary local/technical context, supporting references/dates, assumptions, and unknowns.
- Owners with their actual site/service/action scope, dependencies, timing, and the next update arrangement where required.
- The leader's actual decision or acknowledgment, intended recipient, awareness/action/decision purpose, permitted request/commitment, constraints, and review route.
- The direction's issuer/role, date, source/reference, covered case/recipient, limits, expiry/return conditions, and whether supplied directly, human-confirmed against its source, or unconfirmed.

An assertion that "my leader approved this" is insufficient. Do not invent a reference number: a direct instruction in an approved leader session can be recorded as such. The agent's record does not authenticate identity.

When using a Decision-Ready Escalations brief, retain its sources, risks, unknowns, ownership, and timing, then add the actual human onward instruction separately. The first agent's recommendation or readiness is not a decision or permission. Follow the [shared handoff contract](../../../../README.md#pattern-selection-and-handoff).

## Respond to Readiness

Read both the primary label and the separate blocker/review lists:

| Label | Next human step |
|---|---|
| Use another channel | Follow the identified approved process; retain unresolved evidence and approvals. |
| Needs information | Obtain the listed confirmations or applicable guidance/direction. Reserved reviews remain required afterward. |
| Human review required | Submit the supported draft for the named approval, framing resolution, or return-to-leader review. |
| Ready for sender review | Validate and send yourself only within current traceable direct-send permission and all other applicable controls. |

Missing required information takes precedence over pending approval unless another channel is required. Known uncertainty can remain in a permitted awareness message when its explanation, owner, and next step are established. Missing required profile rules, update arrangements, or newly affected recovery ownership cannot be assumed resolved.

For every new case/recipient or changed profile, identify the expected current version and compare the active rules shown by the agent with the owner's approved complete file, including inherited rules. Record expected/used version, the named human confirmer, date/source, how guidance was supplied, and confirmed/pending/failed status. If you supply the complete current text, explicitly confirm that comparison; the agent cannot do it for you. Pending or failed comparison means Needs information even if the model calls retrieval complete. Correct the guidance and compare again; preserve all reserved reviews. Confirmation verifies neither case facts nor sending authority. An unchanged clarification turn needs no repeat comparison.

If full guidance cannot be obtained through approved access, stop. A citation alone does not settle completeness. Do not ask it to guess preferences from a job title.

## Review and Send

Check the recipient-facing draft against the sources, not just the preparation notes:

- The human profile comparison is recorded as confirmed for the expected/used current version; if the profile or recipient/case changed, repeat it.
- Facts, amounts, dates, uncertainty, risk, and shared responsibility remain accurate and proportionate.
- Chronology does not become unsupported causation or blame. An instruction to hide a material fact needs resolution even when it came from the leader.
- Each owner is limited to the supported site/service/action; another site's recovery and update coverage are not silently assigned.
- The request fits the leader's direction; an awareness message has no invented approval request or new commitment.
- All required reviews are complete. A new material fact, scope, commitment, or request goes back to the leader despite earlier direct-send permission.
- Internal preparation notes, delegation references, private profiles, and unrelated information are excluded unless appropriate and explicitly needed by this recipient.

The sender uses the approved sending channel after completing those checks. An agent readiness label is not evidence verification or an approval artifact.

## Suggest a Guidance Change

Send work-relevant feedback to the guidance owner through the approved process. Keep it separate from current facts and permission. Use the [maintenance procedure](guidance-maintenance.md); do not treat an agent's proposed rule or promise to remember as an adopted shared update.

For practice, use the pattern's [fictional examples](../../README.md#evaluation-criteria). Committee papers and presentation materials require a future specification; this email guide does not establish support for them.
