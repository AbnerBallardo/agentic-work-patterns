# Agentic Work Patterns

Practical ideas for using AI assistants in recurring organizational work. Each pattern explains a problem, shows a fictional example, and gives you instructions to try and adapt.

These are starting points informed by my experience. The public instructions are newly written; they do not reproduce private workplace configurations or promise the same results elsewhere.

## Try a Pattern

1. Read its short explanation and featured example.
2. Copy its instructions into an AI assistant you can use for the exercise.
3. Supply the example's fictional inputs, leaving out the expected answer.
4. Review what the assistant asks and drafts. Adapt the idea to your own work.

Each pattern also includes an optional Microsoft 365 Copilot setup suggestion. No production deployment or tenant-testing project is needed to explore the idea. Use fictional information first; organizational use needs your own permissions and review.

## Pattern Selection and Handoff

Decision-Ready Escalations prepares a brief for a human to decide or act. Executive Communication Preparation applies a leader's actual direction and maintained recipient guidance to onward communication. Either can be used on its own.

Together: **team prepares → leader decides or acknowledges → team prepares executive communication → required human review → human sends**.

Carry forward evidence references/dates, uncertainty, risks, ownership, and timing. Add the actual leader instruction, its issuer/date/source/scope and confirmation status, recipient, permitted commitments, review route, and return conditions. Do not infer approval from a recommendation or transfer another recipient's familiarity or access. See the [fictional handoff](patterns/executive-communication-preparation/examples/pattern-handoff.md); the complete two-pattern workflow has not been evaluated.
